orbit privacy policy

your people stay yours.

effective november 9, 2026 · 100% local

orbit is a personal relationship app that lives on your iPhone. everything you put in it stays there. it doesn't share your personal data with anyone, including us.

read the policy ask a question

there's nothing to opt out of orbit has no servers, no accounts, and no way to see what's on your phone.

the short version

  • stays on your phone

    your orbs, notes, memos and settings live in orbit's private storage on your device.

  • no accounts

    there's nothing to sign up for, and no profile of you anywhere else.

  • no analytics or ads

    orbit doesn't track you and includes no third-party analytics or advertising code.

  • never shared or sold

    we never receive your personal data, so there's nothing for us to share or sell.

what orbit keeps

orbit only stores what you give it. it stays in the app's private storage on your phone until you delete it.

orbs
names, phone numbers, emails, photos, your orbits and systems, and details you add, like how you met, hobbies and notes.
reach-out history
when you last talked to someone and when their next nudge is due.
convo memos
transcripts and the details orbit picks out of them. the audio too, but only if you choose to keep it.
your settings
your name, nudge times, accent color and other preferences.

permissions

every permission is optional. orbit asks when you reach for a feature that needs one, and you can change your answer in iOS settings at any time.

contacts
used to add people quickly and keep linked orbs' photos up to date. orbit reads your contacts and never changes them.
microphone
used only during a convo memo that you start.
speech recognition
turns your recording into text on your phone.
notifications
nudges are scheduled on your phone. there's no push server behind them.

the smart parts run on your phone

transcription
uses apple's on-device speech tools. iOS may download a language pack from apple the first time, but your audio and transcripts don't go with it.
memo insights
uses the on-device apple intelligence model where your phone supports it, and simple local rules where it doesn't. orbit uses no cloud ai.
recordings
audio sits in a temporary folder while you record. if you don't keep it, it's removed. kept audio stays out of iCloud and computer backups.
nudges
worked out on your phone from the reach-out rhythm you set for each orbit.

where orbit meets the rest of your phone

orbit doesn't send your data anywhere. these are the places where iOS, apple, or an app you choose is involved.

device backups

if you use iCloud Backup or back up to a computer, iOS can include orbit's data, as it does for most apps. we can't read those backups. orbit doesn't sync through iCloud, and kept audio is left out.

text, call and email

tapping one of these opens messages, phone or mail. what you send from there is handled by that app and your carrier or email provider.

apple

apple runs the app store and iOS. if you opt in on your iPhone, apple may share crash reports and usage statistics with developers. apple's privacy policy covers this.

this website

this page is hosted on github pages and uses no cookies, analytics or third-party fonts. github may log technical details, such as ip addresses, to run its service.

you're in control

we never receive your data, so there's nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. you can do all of the deleting yourself.

  • delete an orb or memo

    deleting an orb takes its memos and their kept audio with it.

  • erase everything

    go to settings, then your data. this removes all orbs, memos, orbits and systems, and can't be undone.

  • delete the app

    removing orbit removes its data from your phone. backups you've already made are managed separately.

other people's information

orbit holds notes about people you know, so please be thoughtful with them. some places require everyone's consent before a conversation is recorded. check the rules where you are before recording.

children

orbit isn't directed to children under 13, and it doesn't collect personal data from anyone of any age.

changes

if the way orbit handles data changes, we'll update this page and the effective date at the top.

questions

open an issue on github. issues are public, so please leave out personal details.
